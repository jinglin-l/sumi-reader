Why grey text on grey backgrounds fails

Contrast ratio is the relative luminance of the lighter colour plus 0.05, divided by the darker one plus 0.05. Text at #666 on #777 scores about 1.2:1, far under the 4.5:1 that body text needs. Measuring it

Use the formula on each channel after linearising sRGB values. See the fix section or the docs page. Designers often pick low contrast because it looks calm in a mockup, but on a real screen in daylight the words disappear entirely.

L = 0.2126*R + 0.7152*G + 0.0722*B
ratio = (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05)
